Frequently Asked Questions

Why is it cheaper here than in-app?

Apple charges a 30% commission on in-app purchases. When you subscribe on our website, we give you 10% off for your first 3 months — savings we can pass on because we avoid Apple's fees.

Do I need the app?

You can sign up and subscribe right here on the website. Then download SiteStream from the App Store and sign in with the same email — your subscription activates instantly.

What is SiteStream Intelligence?

Intelligence is our analytics platform that scores consultant performance, monitors project health, and predicts risk across your portfolio. It runs on intelligence.sitestream.au as a web dashboard — separate from the mobile app.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Cancel with one click from your account settings or email us. No lock-in contracts, no hidden fees.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) and Apple Pay/Google Pay via Stripe's secure checkout.
